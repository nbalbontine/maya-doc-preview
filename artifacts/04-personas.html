3. User personas

UP-PM — Priya, project manager

Segment: GC project team, or subcontractor PM across many projects · Relevant information: already has access to ProjectSight, Connect and Maya captures; the early-adopter profile the workshop named (WS-D2).

"I know the answer is somewhere — an email, a spec, a photo. Finding it is my afternoon." (illustrative, from WS-D1)

Problems

  • "I can't see what changed on site between two dates without opening both captures and scanning every station." (MP-02)
  • "I can't tell which open RFIs, punch items and BCF topics could be closed without sending someone to check each one." (MP-01)
  • "I don't know how complete an area is against schedule and design until a daily report arrives." (MP-03)
  • "Answering one question means logging into five Trimble products." (MP-09)
  • "I can only look at captures one project at a time." (MP-10)
  • "I can't trust an AI answer I can't check." (MP-13)

Background story Priya runs a mid-size commercial fit-out. Her week is built around the coordination meeting where the owner, designers and trades ask what has changed and what is outstanding. The captures are there, but she answers from the daily reports and what the superintendent tells her, because opening two captures side by side for every area takes too long. At a subcontractor, the same role spans many projects at once (WS-D2).

Goals

  • Be: the person in the meeting who has the answer and the proof.
  • Do: close outstanding items, keep the schedule, catch problems before they cost money.
  • Feel: in control of the project without being on site every day.

Behaviors Works from the office and site cabin; lives in ProjectSight and email; skims reports; trusts photos over prose.

Workflows owned: WF-01, WF-02, WF-04, WF-09

Evidence: WS-D1, WS-D2, V1-PRD · Status: asserted · Validation: VB-15

UP-VDC — Dev, VDC / digital engineer

Segment: GC digital delivery team · Relevant information: manages federated models and BIM metadata; compares design with reality (WS-D2).

"By the time I see the deviation, it's already a change order." (illustrative)

Problems

  • "I find out that what was built differs from the model only after it has become a change order." (MP-05)
  • "I type zones and locations into Excel by hand before I can report status by zone." (MP-12)

Background story Dev keeps the models current and colours model elements by progress for the PM. Comparing a capture to the model is manual; zones are drawn by hand.

Goals

  • Be: the team's source of truth for as-built versus design.
  • Do: catch deviations while they are cheap to fix.
  • Feel: confident the model reflects the site.

Behaviors Desktop-heavy; Connect and model viewers; comfortable with filters and property sets (V1-PRD).

Workflows owned: WF-08 (and supports WF-02, WF-04)

Evidence: WS-D2, WS-SH, V1-PRD · Status: asserted · Validation: VB-16

UP-QS — Quinn, quantity surveyor

Segment: subcontractor or GC commercial team · Relevant information: volumes, quantities, percent complete for payment (WS-D2).

"A steel detailer gets paid for how much weight of steel has been erected." (paraphrase, WS-D2)

Problems

  • "I float labour and materials until I can prove what was installed, and assembling that proof delays payment." (MP-04)

Background story Quinn prepares the monthly payment application against the schedule of values. The evidence is photos and site notes pulled together by hand.

Goals

  • Be: the person whose application gets approved first time.
  • Do: prove installed quantities with evidence the GC accepts.
  • Feel: not exposed when a figure is challenged.

Behaviors Spreadsheet-first; monthly rhythm; needs exportable evidence.

Workflows owned: WF-05

Evidence: WS-D1, WS-D2, V1-PRD · Status: asserted · Validation: VB-17

UP-SUPER — Sam, superintendent

Segment: GC site management · Relevant information: four-week lookahead, crew allocation, rework prevention (WS-D2).

"What's the status of zone A, what's the status of zone B? I need to send the crew to floor two zone five." (paraphrase, WS-D2)

Problems

  • "Checking that snags and punch items were actually fixed means walking the same route again." (MP-07)
  • "Nobody systematically reviews site imagery for safety issues." (MP-08)

Background story Sam is on site all day. He wants short answers by zone, on his phone, and has no time for a chat window.

Goals

  • Be: ahead of the next problem.
  • Do: get crews to the right zone; close snags once.
  • Feel: that the office sees what he sees.

Behaviors Mobile; walks the site; reads maps, not paragraphs.

Workflows owned: WF-06, WF-07

Evidence: WS-D2 · Status: asserted · Validation: VB-18

UP-FIELD — Femi, field engineer (capture operator)

Segment: site team · Relevant information: walks with the 360 camera in ProjectSight Mobile (V1-PRD).

"I only find out I missed the east stair when someone asks for it." (illustrative)

Problems

  • "I miss areas on my site walk and only find out when someone asks for a view that isn't there." (MP-06)

Background story Femi captures weekly. Nobody tells him what was missed until it is too late to re-walk.

Goals

  • Be: reliable.
  • Do: capture everything needed in one walk.
  • Feel: that the walk was worth it.

Behaviors Mobile; follows a habitual route.

Workflows owned: WF-03

Evidence: WS-D1, WS-D3, WS-SH · Status: asserted · Validation: VB-19

UP-SUPPORT — Sasha, Trimble support engineer (internal)

Goals
