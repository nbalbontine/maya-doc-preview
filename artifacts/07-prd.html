Product Requirements Document — Maya Agentic Platform (MAP) v0.1

What will we build? Scope, requirements, epics. · Owner: product definer

Confidential and subject to change. For internal use only. No external commitments can be made based on this document.

Company: Trimble · Product: Maya (MAP) · Author: product definer (generated draft) · Version: 0.1.0

1. Overview of the target release

  • Goal: ship a Maya V2 agent that answers "what changed", "what can I close" and "are we on track" from existing capture, ProjectSight and Connect data, with visible evidence, inside the products people already use.
  • Market forces: LLMs have reset customer expectations for AI assistance (WS-D1); OpenSpace has already shown an agent-to-agent RFI/punch resolution demo (WS-D1, WS-D2); v1 adoption (~1,000 captures, 6M sq ft, TEL) proves the capture side, not the agent side.
  • Driven by: content — first usable workflow slice, not a fixed date. Window: short term, to capture real feedback before scope hardens (WS-D1 D-09). > TODO: calendar date — owner Nathan Patton.
  • Related artifacts: 01-brief.md · 02-market-problems.md · 03-competitive-landscape.md · 04-personas.md · 05-workflow-map.md · 06-touchpoint-map.md.

4. Scope

4a. In scope

The five workflow epics and three enablers in §7, covering WF-01 through WF-10 from 05-workflow-map.md.

4b. Out of scope / non-goals

  • A general-purpose chatbot experience (brief 2c).
  • Maya making or executing decisions in source systems (brief 2c, principle 1).
  • New identity, permissions or project-record storage inside Maya (brief 2c).
  • Role-branching agent behaviour (brief 2c; short-term agent is user-agnostic).
  • New capture-time vision models (segmentation, safety detection) beyond what exists in prototype, until the workflows they'd serve are validated.
  • Pricing and packaging mechanics for the AI tier (brief §3, N-01) — tracked, not specified, here.

4c. Boundaries and principles (from the brief)

All ten principles in 01-brief.md §2d apply to every requirement below. Most load-bearing here: evidence-not-decisions (1), decline with a path to yes (2), source systems win (4), visual first (6), and same answer from every front door (7).

5. Requirements (grouped, prioritized)

GROUP 1 — W-CHANGE — See and cover change over time

R-CHANGE-01 — Change between two dates UP-PM has "I can't see what changed on site between two dates without opening both captures and scanning every station" when preparing for a coordination meeting, weekly per project (frequency > TODO: validate, VB-01). Traces: MP-02 · WF-02 · TP-03, TP-04, TP-07, TP-08, TP-14 Priority: 4 × 4 = 16 (market evidence: 1)

R-CHANGE-02 — Capture coverage UP-FIELD has "I miss areas on my site walk and only find out when someone asks for a view that isn't there" every capture walk. Traces: MP-06 · WF-03 · TP-03, TP-05, TP-09, TP-14 Priority: 3 × 3 = 9 (market evidence: 0)

GROUP 2 — W-CLOSEOUT — Close items with evidence

R-CLOSEOUT-01 — Which open items can close UP-PM has "I can't tell which open RFIs, punch items and BCF topics could be closed without sending someone to check each one" during weekly item review (frequency > TODO: validate, VB-02). Traces: MP-01 · WF-01 · TP-02, TP-07, TP-11, TP-12, TP-14 Priority: 3 × 4 = 12 (market evidence: 0)

R-CLOSEOUT-02 — Verify fixes (reinspection queue) UP-SUPER asks what punch is ready for me to sign off? when reviewing punch awaiting reinspection in ProjectSight — before/after evidence, then close or push back — instead of re-walking every location. Not R-CLOSEOUT-01: that requirement is open RFIs/punch/BCF and "could I close?" with a single latest image, not the reinspection queue. Traces: MP-07 · WF-06 · TP-03, TP-07, TP-08, TP-12, TP-14 Priority: 1 × 3 = 3 (market evidence: 0)

GROUP 3 — W-PROGRESS — Progress and payment evidence

R-PROGRESS-01 — Completeness against plan UP-PM has "I don't know how complete an area is against schedule and design until a daily report arrives, and those are late and inaccurate" during weekly progress review. Traces: MP-03 · WF-04 · TP-02, TP-03, TP-06, TP-07, TP-11, TP-14 Priority: 3 × 4 = 12 (market evidence: 0)

R-PROGRESS-02 — Payment evidence UP-QS has "I float labour and materials until I can prove what was installed, and assembling that proof delays payment" every monthly payment application. Traces: MP-04 · WF-05 · TP-02, TP-07, TP-13, TP-14 Priority: 3 × 4 = 12 (market evidence: 0)

R-PROGRESS-03 — Zones without manual entry UP-VDC has "I type zones and locations into Excel by hand before I can report status by zone" every time zones are needed for a report. Traces: MP-12 · WF-04 · TP-06 Priority: 1 × 2 = 2 (market evidence: 0)

GROUP 4 — W-SIGNALS — Deviation and safety signals

R-SIGNALS-01 — Deviation from design UP-VDC has "I find out that what was built differs from the model only after it has become a change order" after each capture of an active area. Traces: MP-05 · WF-08 · TP-02, TP-03, TP-07, TP-08, TP-12, TP-14 Priority: 2 × 4 = 8 (market evidence: 0)

R-SIGNALS-02 — Safety imagery review UP-SUPER has "nobody systematically reviews site imagery for safety issues like missing PPE or fall protection" during daily or weekly safety review. Traces: MP-08 · WF-07 · TP-03, TP-07, TP-12, TP-14 Priority: 1 × 3 = 6 (market evidence: 1)

Caution: liability if a decline is read as a safety clearance (see WS-D2; workflow role boundary in 05-workflow-map.md WF-07).

GROUP 5 — W-ACCESS — Reach Maya and diagnose access issues

R-ACCESS-01 — One question, one place UP-PM has "answering one question means logging into five Trimble products and sifting through email" every time a cross-system question comes up. Traces: MP-09 · WF-09 · TP-01, TP-02, TP-14 Priority: 2 × 3 = 6 (market evidence: 0)

R-ACCESS-02 — Cross-project view BP-SUB (via UP-PM) has "I work across many projects, but I can only look at captures one project at a time" every time they check status across their portfolio. Traces: MP-10 · WF-09 · TP-01, TP-14 Priority: 1 × 3 = 6 (market evidence: 1)

R-ACCESS-03 — Diagnose a failed capture UP-SUPPORT has "diagnosing a failed capture takes an email chain between the customer, support and engineering" every time a customer reports a failure. Traces: MP-11 · WF-10 · TP-10, TP-14 Priority: 2 × 3 = 6 (market evidence: 0)

Enablers (no Evidence × Impact score)

R-ACCESS-ENB-01 — P-ACCESS: reach Maya, ProjectSight and Connect data safely Enables: R-CHANGE-01, R-CHANGE-02, R-CLOSEOUT-01, R-CLOSEOUT-02, R-PROGRESS-01, R-PROGRESS-02, R-SIGNALS-01, R-SIGNALS-02, R-ACCESS-01, R-ACCESS-02, R-ACCESS-03 (every workflow requirement reads through the Maya API/MCP and, for source-system data, through their APIs under authorization). 6. Non-functional requirements

ID Requirement Traces Measure
NFR-01 Every Maya answer states what it is based on (captures, dates, records) and offers feedback. MP-13, principle 9 100% of answers carry a feedback control and at least one cited source
NFR-02 Every answering capability declines with a path to yes when data is insufficient, rather than guessing. MP-13, principle 2 0 fabricated answers in eval question bank (WS-D1 domain bank)
NFR-03 The agent never returns data the requesting user could not see in the source system. brief principle 8, AQ-01/AQ-02 0 authorization escapes in security review
NFR-04 Expensive queries (image scans) run only after a coverage check confirms data exists for the scope. principle 3, Q-10 Coverage check present in every capture-scanning tool call
NFR-05 Counts, positions, dates and scores are computed deterministically; the model explains, it does not compute. principle 5, WS-SH hallucination incident 0 numeric hallucinations in eval question bank
NFR-06 Spatial answers render on the drawing or in the viewer, not as text-only. principle 6 Every WF-02/WF-03/WF-04/WF-08 answer includes a visual surface
NFR-07 The same question gives the same answer from every front door it is available on. principle 7 Parity test across TP-01, TP-02, TP-03
NFR-08 Maya never writes to a source-system record without explicit human confirmation. brief principle 1, WS-D1 0 unconfirmed writes in audit log
NFR-09 Data privacy and security match Trimble's existing commitments (GDPR, SOC 2) as Maya reads more source-system data. V1-PRD Carried from v1; no regression
