2. Cross-cutting tensions

  • Persona-led design, user-agnostic agent. Workflows are written per persona, but the short-term agent does not change behaviour by role (WS-D2 D-12). A superintendent and a PM asking the same question get the same answer. Role shows up in telemetry, not in branching.
  • Evidence, not decisions. Every "With Maya" sequence ends at a human checkpoint. Maya never closes, approves or edits a record in a source system on its own (WS-D1, brief principle 1).
  • Source systems win. RFIs, punch items and daily reports stay in ProjectSight; BCF topics in Connect. Maya reads them through their APIs and links back (D-06).
  • Visual first versus Assist today. Trimble Assist is the initial front end and renders text-heavy output (WS-D3). Workflows assume spatial answers appear on the drawing or in the viewer; where only Assist chat is available, the answer must still link to the spatial view.
  • Cost. Deep image queries are expensive (Q-10); every workflow that scans captures starts with a coverage check.
  • WF-01 versus WF-06. Both can show punch with photos and agree/disagree, but WF-01 targets open RFIs, punch and BCF ("could I close?" — MP-01); WF-06 targets punch in reinspection-pending status only ("ready to sign off?" — before/after evidence, MP-07). Intent and ProjectSight status cohort must route the ask; see docs/raw/Design Step-by-Step Workflows.md (WF-06 comparison table).

3. Workflows

WF-02 — See what changed on site between two dates

  • Persona: UP-PM · Also involved: UP-VDC, UP-SUPER
  • Trigger: preparing for the weekly coordination meeting; an owner or trade asks "what's happened on level 3 since the 12th?" Weekly, per project. > TODO: validate frequency (VB-01).
  • Problems addressed: MP-02

Today

  1. Open ProjectSight, open the Maya viewer — ProjectSight Web, viewer — several clicks per floor.
  2. Pick two capture dates in split screen — viewer — has to know which dates cover the area.
  3. Step through stations one by one comparing images — viewer — slow; easy to miss changes.
  4. Write up what changed in email or meeting notes — email — no link back to the evidence.

With Maya

  1. Asks "what changed on level 3 between 12 and 26 September?" — Priya — Assist in ProjectSight or in the viewer.
  2. Checks both dates have captures covering the area; if not, declines with what is missing — Maya — decline message.
  3. Computes change per station and zone deterministically — Maya — (no surface; backend).
  4. Shows the change heat map on the floor plan: major, moderate, none — Maya — heat-map overlay in viewer.
  5. Summarises the largest changes with before/after images and dates — Maya — evidence report.
  6. Clicks a zone to open the before/after pair in split screen — Priya — viewer split screen.
  7. Rates the answer — Priya — feedback control.

Human checkpoint: Priya decides what the change means and what to do; she sees the heat map, the image pairs and the dates for every claim. Maya's role boundary: does not judge whether the change is correct, on schedule or a deviation; that is WF-04 and WF-08. Data needed: captures, stations, poses and drawing alignment (Maya API via P-ACCESS); drawing revisions for both dates. Decline and path to yes: "Level 3 has no capture between 20 and 26 September. The nearest is 19 September. Compare 12 and 19 September instead, or capture level 3 again." Evidence status: signal · Validation: VB-01

WF-03 — Check what my walk covered and what to capture next

  • Persona: UP-FIELD · Also involved: UP-PM
  • Trigger: after uploading a capture; before the next weekly walk.
  • Problems addressed: MP-06

Today

  1. Walks the habitual route with the 360 camera — ProjectSight Mobile — no feedback on gaps.
  2. Uploads; waits for processing — Atlas — no coverage summary.
  3. Hears weeks later that an area has no capture — email — too late to re-walk.

With Maya

  1. Asks "what did my walk miss on level 2?" or receives a coverage summary after processing — Femi — Assist, or scheduled notification.
  2. Shows captured versus not-captured areas on the floor plan for the chosen period — Maya — coverage overlay.
  3. Lists areas not captured for more than a chosen number of days — Maya — evidence report.
  4. Plans the next walk — Femi — (off-system).

Human checkpoint: Femi decides the route; Maya only shows coverage. Maya's role boundary: does not schedule people or walks (APE's territory). Data needed: station positions per capture and drawing (Maya API). Decline and path to yes: if the drawing is not aligned, "This capture isn't aligned to a drawing yet, so coverage can't be shown. Align it in ProjectSight, or wait for processing to finish." Evidence status: asserted · Validation: VB-06

WF-01 — Find which open items I can close

  • Persona: UP-PM · Also involved: UP-SUPER
  • Trigger: weekly item review; preparing for handover. > TODO: validate frequency (VB-02).
  • Problems addressed: MP-01

Today

  1. Lists open RFIs and punch items in ProjectSight and BCF topics in Connect — two products — separate lists.
  2. For each, works out where on site it is — drawings, specs — slow.
  3. Opens captures near that location, or sends someone to look — viewer or site walk — site time per item.
  4. Closes items one by one — ProjectSight, Connect — no evidence attached.

With Maya

  1. Asks "which open items on level 2 could I close?" — Priya — Assist.
  2. Gathers open RFIs, punch items and BCF topics with locations — Maya — (backend, via source-system APIs).
  3. Checks recent capture coverage for each item location — Maya — (backend).
  4. Classifies each: likely resolved on capture, not visible — needs a capture, needs action outside reality capture — Maya — evidence report.
  5. For likely resolved, shows the latest image at the item location with its date and the item text — Maya — evidence report, viewer.
  6. For not visible, recommends a targeted capture ("photograph the valve tag in riser 2") — Maya — decline with path to yes.
  7. Accepts or rejects each classification — Priya — human checkpoint in chat.
  8. Opens accepted items in their home product to close them — Priya — link-out to ProjectSight or Connect.
  9. Rates the answer — Priya — feedback control.

Human checkpoint: accept or reject per item; closing happens in the source system by the person. Maya's role boundary: never closes or edits the record (short term); never answers an RFI's design question. Data needed: ProjectSight RFIs and punch items, Connect BCF topics (their MCP/APIs through P-ACCESS); item locations; captures and stations. Decline and path to yes: items with no location → "I can't place this item on a drawing; add a location in ProjectSight." Items with no recent capture → recommended capture. Evidence status: asserted · Validation: VB-02

WF-06 — Confirm snags and punch items were fixed

  • Persona: UP-SUPER · Also involved: UP-PM
  • Trigger: punch items in the project's reinspection queue (e.g. Pending Reinspection or Pending Approval — status names vary by project); after a trade or assignee reports work complete, before Sam closes or sends items back.
  • Problems addressed: MP-07

Today

  1. Opens the punch list filtered to items awaiting reinspection — ProjectSight — separate from "open defects."
  2. Walks the route again to verify each reported fix — on foot — repeated walk.
  3. In ProjectSight Mobile, closes items that look good or pushes back to Open with a comment when they do not.

With Maya

  1. Asks "what punch is ready for me to sign off?" (Maya gathers the project's reinspection-pending queue; user does not name PS status labels) — Sam — Assist (mobile).
  2. Gathers items in the configured reinspection status (or equivalent workflow step), with location and the date the item entered that status — Maya — (backend; uses ProjectSight workflowStates / query, not a hard-coded Resolved label).
  3. Compares each item's location before and after that reinspection-request date — Maya — evidence report with image pairs (card stack).
  4. Accepts or rejects each card — Sam — human checkpoint in chat (judgement only; no record writes).
  5. Link-out to ProjectSight — Sam — approve and close on agree, or push back (e.g. status to Open, resolution comment) on disagree.

Human checkpoint: Sam decides whether someone else's "done" call still makes sense; agree/disagree is his verdict, not Maya's. Maya's role boundary: no pass/fail judgement on workmanship quality; never changes punch status, comments, or close/push-back in ProjectSight. Data needed: punch items in reinspection-pending status (project-specific), status-change or reinspection-request date, locations; captures before and after that date; optional assignee completion flags for context. Decline and path to yes: no capture after the reinspection-request date → "Capture the item location after the fix was reported." Default cohort excludes Closed items unless the user explicitly asks for a post-close audit (different question). Evidence status: asserted · Validation: VB-12

WF-04 — Check progress against plan by zone

  • Persona: UP-PM · Also involved: UP-SUPER, UP-VDC
  • Trigger: weekly progress review; lookahead planning.
  • Problems addressed: MP-03, MP-12

Today

  1. Reads daily reports — ProjectSight — late and inaccurate (WS-D2).
  2. Asks the superintendent — phone — subjective.
  3. Updates the schedule or model colours by hand — scheduling tool, Connect — manual zones in Excel (MP-12).

With Maya

  1. Asks "is level 3 on track?" — Priya — Assist.
  2. Reads planned activities for the zone and period — Maya — (from schedule source, APE or ProjectSight; unresolved).
  3. Compares latest captures with planned state per zone — Maya — progress-to-plan heat map.
  4. Explains ahead / on / behind per zone with images — Maya — evidence report.
  5. Decides what to do — Priya — (APE for schedule recommendations).

Human checkpoint: Priya interprets; schedule changes go through APE or the scheduler. Maya's role boundary: no schedule recommendations (APE's role). Data needed: schedule by zone (source open), zones or location breakdown structure (P-SPATIAL), captures. Decline and path to yes: no schedule linked → "I can show what changed, but not whether it is on plan. Link the schedule for this project." Evidence status: asserted · Validation: VB-03, VB-13

WF-05 — Prove installed work for a payment application

  • Persona: UP-QS · Also involved: BP-SUB, the GC's QS
  • Trigger: monthly payment application.
  • Problems addressed: MP-04

Today

  1. Collects photos and site notes for each line of the schedule of values — email, phone — manual.
  2. Assembles an evidence pack — spreadsheet — slow.
  3. Answers challenges from the GC — email — delays payment.

With Maya

  1. Asks "show evidence for steel installed on levels 2–4 this month" — Quinn — Assist.
  2. Finds captures in the period and area — Maya — coverage check.
  3. Produces an evidence pack with dated images per area — Maya — evidence report (exportable).
  4. Attaches it to the application — Quinn — (Trimble Pay or off-system; open).

Human checkpoint: Quinn decides what to claim. Maya's role boundary: does not compute quantities or approve claims (short term). Data needed: captures, zones; schedule of values (Trimble Pay, unconfirmed). Decline and path to yes: no capture of an area in the period → names the areas and dates missing. Evidence status: asserted · Validation: VB-04

WF-08 — Catch deviations from design before they become change orders

  • Persona: UP-VDC · Also involved: UP-PM
  • Trigger: after each capture of an active area.
  • Problems addressed: MP-05

Today

  1. Compares capture to model when someone raises a concern — viewer split screen with model — reactive.

With Maya

  1. Flags areas where the capture differs from the model — Maya — evidence report, viewer.
  2. Reviews and raises an RFI or BCF topic if real — Dev — link-out.

Human checkpoint: Dev decides whether it is a deviation. Maya's role boundary: no change-order prediction as fact; flags only. Data needed: model alignment to capture (P-SPATIAL). Decline and path to yes: no aligned model → "Align the model to this drawing to compare." Evidence status: asserted · Validation: VB-07

WF-07 — Review recent imagery for safety issues

  • Persona: UP-SUPER
  • Trigger: daily or weekly safety review.
  • Problems addressed: MP-08

Today

  1. Safety walk and checklist — ProjectSight checklists — imagery not reviewed.

With Maya

  1. Flags images with possible PPE or fall-protection issues — Maya — evidence report.
  2. Reviews each and drafts an incident report if real — Sam — link-out to ProjectSight.

Human checkpoint: Sam decides; Maya never files a safety report on its own. Maya's role boundary: a missed issue must never be presented as "no issues found". Liability open. Data needed: recent images; detection capability (not built). Decline and path to yes: states the images reviewed and the period, not a safety clearance. Evidence status: signal · Validation: VB-08

WF-09 — Ask Maya from where I already work, across projects

  • Persona: UP-PM · Also involved: BP-SUB
  • Trigger: any question; the product the persona has open.
  • Problems addressed: MP-09, MP-10

Today

  1. Logs into ProjectSight, Connect, Maya viewer and others to assemble one answer — five logins (WS-D1).
  2. Repeats per project — one project at a time (WS-D2).

With Maya

  1. Asks from Assist in the product already open, or from standalone Assist — Priya — any front door.
  2. Maya answers with the same result from any front door — Maya.
  3. Asks across projects: "which of my projects had no capture this week?" — Priya — Assist.

Human checkpoint: n/a (question answering). Maya's role boundary: only projects the user can access. Data needed: projects the user can access (Trimble ID, BFF). Decline and path to yes: a project the user cannot access → "You don't have access to that project; ask its admin." Evidence status: asserted · Validation: VB-09, VB-10

WF-10 — Diagnose a failed capture

  • Persona: UP-SUPPORT
  • Trigger: customer reports a capture did not process.
  • Problems addressed: MP-11

Today

  1. Emails the customer for details — email.
  2. Checks SKIT for access and processing status — SKIT.
  3. Escalates to engineering — email chain.

With Maya

  1. Asks "why did this capture fail?" in SKIT — Sasha — Assist in SKIT.
  2. Reads access, upload and processing status and known failure causes — Maya — evidence report.
  3. Tells the customer the fix or escalates with the evidence attached — Sasha.

Human checkpoint: Sasha decides the response. Maya's role boundary: read-only; no reprocessing on its own. Data needed: SKIT access and processing records (Maya MCP in SKIT, prototype). Decline and path to yes: unknown failure → escalation note with everything gathered. Evidence status: asserted · Validation: VB-11
